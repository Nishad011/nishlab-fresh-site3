Who We Are

Engineering Learning With a Career Focus

Nishlab Creations brings design, simulation, automation and career preparation into one structured learning ecosystem.

Our Mission

To make engineering software education more practical, structured and career-oriented. Students should not finish a course knowing only commands; they should be able to use those tools to create engineering work and explain it confidently.

“Learn the tool. Build the skill. Create the project. Become industry ready.”

What Makes Us Different

Our curriculum connects CAD, CAE, automation and career preparation. Each technical module has a practical capstone, while the final career module focuses on resume, portfolio, LinkedIn and interview preparation.

The complete professional program is structured as 204 hours across 102 training days, with 2 hours of structured training per day.
