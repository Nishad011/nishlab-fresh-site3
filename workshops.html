AIKTC School of Engineering

Our workshop at AIKTC School of Engineering, Department of Mechanical Engineering, brought students together for an interactive engineering learning session, with live presentation, classroom interaction and practical discussion.

From Classroom to Industry

The workshop format is designed to make engineering software more understandable and relevant. We connect CAD/CAM/CAE concepts with how engineers think about design, documentation, modelling and professional skills.

What Students Experience

Students participate in a visual presentation, instructor-led explanation, questions and discussion. The objective is to create awareness of modern engineering workflows and encourage students to build practical software skills alongside their academic curriculum.
