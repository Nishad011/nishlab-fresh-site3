01

AutoCAD Professional

Industry-standard 2D drafting, documentation, layouts, plotting and introductory 3D modelling.

24 Hours · 12 Days

What You'll Learn

  • Precision drawing with coordinate systems, OSNAP and polar tracking
  • Trim, extend, offset, array and stretch
  • Layers, properties and CAD standards management
  • Annotation, dimensioning and GD&T-ready drawings
  • Blocks, attributes, dynamic blocks and title blocks
  • XREF and Sheet Set management
  • Model space, paper space, viewports, CTB/STB plotting and PDF publishing
  • Introductory 3D solids: extrude, revolve, sweep, loft and Boolean operations

Software & Tools

AutoCAD, DWG/DXF drafting standards, Sheet Set Manager and PDF publishing.

Best Suited For

Freshers and engineers who need production-grade 2D drafting and documentation skills.

04

ANSYS Workbench Professional

FEA fundamentals, static structural, contact, modal, thermal, fatigue, CFD introduction and reporting.

36 Hours · 18 Days

What You'll Learn

  • FEA workflow, geometry cleanup, named selections and mid-surfacing
  • Meshing: sizing, inflation, quality metrics and convergence study
  • Static structural analysis, supports, loads and solver settings
  • Stress/deformation post-processing and safety factor evaluation
  • Contact, modal and harmonic response basics
  • Steady-state/transient thermal and thermal-structural coupling
  • Fatigue life prediction and design validation
  • Introductory CFD with Fluent: meshing, solving and post-processing

Software & Tools

ANSYS Workbench, DesignModeler/SpaceClaim and ANSYS Fluent.

Best Suited For

Design engineers who need to validate parts under load, heat, vibration or fluid flow.
